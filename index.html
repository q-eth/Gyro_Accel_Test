The dot shows where the measured acceleration vector points in the X/Y plane of the phone. Lying flat, gravity is all on Z (+1 g) and the dot sits in the center. Tilt the phone and gravity shifts onto X and Y. The inner ring is 0.5 g, the outer ring 1 g, and the sphere edge 1.5 g.

X Y Z horizontal reference

The gyroscope measures angular velocity (°/s). The browser fuses it with the accelerometer (and magnetometer, if the phone has one) to get the orientation angles in degrees. Yaw (Z) is often measured from an arbitrary starting direction, so the 3D view leaves it out unless you switch it on.

Axes follow the W3C DeviceOrientation spec: X points to the right edge of the screen, Y to the top edge, Z out of the screen toward you.
